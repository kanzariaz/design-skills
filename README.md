# Skills

Product and Design workflow skills for Claude Code. Explore an idea freely
without being rushed into building.

**Site:** https://kanzariaz.github.io/skills

## Install

```bash
npx skills add kanzariaz/skills --skill=list-it
npx skills add kanzariaz/skills --skill=explore
npx skills add kanzariaz/skills --skill=mock-it
```

Or copy any `skills/<name>/SKILL.md` into `~/.claude/skills/<name>/SKILL.md`
(global) or `.claude/skills/<name>/SKILL.md` in a project.

## The skills

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| **list-it** | Collect changes as a numbered list; build nothing until you say "execute" |
| **explore** | A parallel sandbox lane — options, mockups, trade-offs; leaks nothing into your queue |
| **mock-it** | 2–4 labeled design variants rendered inline, in your project's real design tokens |

## How they fit together

`list-it` is the conveyor belt: decided work, queued, executed only on your
word. `explore` is the sandbox that runs beside it: ideas get kicked around
without landing on the list, in your code, or in your ledgers. `mock-it` is the
sketchpad both lanes reach for when a decision needs to be *seen* rather than
described. The shared discipline: **nothing gets built until you say
"execute."**
